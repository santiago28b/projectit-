# Project It

A screening platform where Companies give Candidates short, realistic Projects, and AI matches Candidates to Projects so Companies find people who can show the skills they need.

## People and organizations

**Candidate**:
A person who works on and submits Projects to show what they can do.
_Avoid_: Applicant, job seeker, employee, user

**Company**:
An organization that publishes Projects and reviews the Submissions to them.
_Avoid_: Employer, client

**Job**:
An open role at a Company, described by the skills it requires. It is linked to Projects by matching skills, not by ownership.
_Avoid_: Job opening, posting, position

## Projects

**Project**:
A short (about 1–2 hour) realistic task, published by one Company or by Project It. Only its Company or its Sponsors review Submissions to it.
_Avoid_: Challenge, assignment, take-home, contest

**Platform Project**:
A Project written by Project It, open to any Candidate at any time, which Companies can Sponsor.
_Avoid_: Template, open project

**Sponsor**:
A Company that attaches its name to a Platform Project and gains the right to review its Submissions.

**Visibility**:
Who can see and start a Project: public (shown in the Marketplace), or restricted to a university, a region, or invited Candidates only.
_Avoid_: Access, privacy

**Invitation**:
A Company's request for a specific Candidate to complete a non-public Project.

**Marketplace**:
The place where Candidates browse the public Projects they're eligible for.
_Avoid_: Job board

## Submissions and review

**Submission**:
A Candidate's finished work on a Project, which always includes a Walkthrough. A Candidate gets one Submission per Project.

**Walkthrough**:
A short (about 2–5 minute) required video where the Candidate explains their approach, decisions, and trade-offs, to prove they understand their own work.
_Avoid_: Demo video, explanation video

**Evidence**:
How strongly a Candidate has shown a skill (strong, partial, not shown, not assessed), labeled AI-assessed or Company-reviewed. A Company-reviewed level overrides the AI's. A Candidate's Evidence for a skill is their strongest level across all Submissions.
_Avoid_: Score, match percentage

**Evaluation**:
A human reviewer's judgment of a Submission against the Project's Rubric, which may override the Evidence.

**Rubric**:
The categories a Project's Submissions are judged on.

**Shortlist**:
The Candidates a Company has picked to interview.

## Matching

**Match**:
An AI-suggested pairing between a Candidate and a Project, or between a Job and a Candidate or Project. It always comes with the reasons for it, never a percentage.
_Avoid_: Score, fit %, recommendation score
